## The house rule

The site carries house ads and its insurer looks at it. In February the insurer
sent a letter about the board hosting what reads as professional advice, and the
rule that came out of it is the one in the board's sidebar: **no specific legal,
tax or filing instructions.** Talking about what a thing is, what it cost, how it
went — all fine. A reply that walks somebody through a specific filing — this
exact form, to this office, by this date — is what a professional is for, and it
comes off.

The desk cannot keep the reply and take the instruction out of it — Postmill
lets a person edit their own reply and lets the site admin edit anybody's, and
the desk is neither, so a reply that crosses the line comes off whole. Removals
go through the delete control with a reason typed on the form, which is what
shows up on the board's moderation log.
